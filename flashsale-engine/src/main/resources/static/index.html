    What happens behind the scenes

    1. Hold: a Redis Lua script grabs the seat atomically, with a 5-minute TTL.
    2. Pay: the booking and a payment request are written in one MongoDB transaction (outbox), then sent over Kafka.
    3. Confirm or compensate: a success sells the seat; a decline releases it; a late payment is refunded.
    4. Live: every change is pushed to all browsers over WebSocket.
    5. Email: a notification service on Kafka sends your ticket (with a calendar invite), declines, refunds and a reminder the day before.
